Click on the soma side indicator to se…`). All 11 V7a types use a **link** title instead, of the form `title="View all <type> (N cells)"`, with separate L/R soma-link titles. VERIFIED (types.html, grep over the whole file).
- Example card header, verbatim (offset ≈16,342,079): `<a title="View all ORN_DM4 (32 cells)" class=neuron-name-link href=types/ORN_DM4.html>ORN_DM4</a> … title="View ORN_DM4 right side (15 cells)" … title="View ORN_DM4 left side (16 cells)"`. VERIFIED.
- A parser that only matches "has N cells" will find **zero** of the V7a types. INFERRED (follows from the counts above).

### 2c. Things that block "drive all ORN_<glom> cells, read that glomerulus's uniglomerular PNs" (measured facts only)

1. **The title format differs from the brief.** None of the 11 types uses "has N cells". VERIFIED.
2. **VM7 is split.** There is no `ORN_VM7` and no `VM7_*PN`. The types are ORN_VM7d (36) / ORN_VM7v (25) and VM7d_adPN (7) / VM7v_adPN (3). A literal `ORN_VM7` match finds 0 cells, and `ORN_VM7*` merges two types. VERIFIED.
3. **DM4 has two uniglomerular PN types, and one is GABAergic.** DM4_vPN is listed as "gaba" (86.5% CL). Its largest input is DM4_adPN (28.7%), while ORN_DM4 supplies 10.1%. VERIFIED.
4. **ORN sides are incomplete.** ORN_DM4 and ORN_DL5 each have 1 body with `rootSide` "unknown". ORN cards have no somaSide, so L/R comes from rootSide. VERIFIED.
5. **Some ORN bodies are "RT Hard to trace".** DM4 has 2, DL5 1, VM7v 2 and DM1 2. VERIFIED. Their effect on input or output counts was not measured: CAN'T VERIFY.
6. **PN counts are small and asymmetric.** There is 1 cell per side for DM4_adPN, DM4_vPN, DL5_adPN and DM1_lPN. VM7d_adPN is 3 L / 4 R and VM7v_adPN is 2 L / 1 R. VERIFIED.
7. **Cross-glomerular ORN input.** ORN_DL2v supplies 5.3% of DM4_adPN's input. VERIFIED.
8. **Multiglomerular PNs that get ORN input are not reachable by name.** VERIFIED.
9. **FLYCNS data coverage.** In the 10-18 check, `data/gen6_phaseA_celltypes.json` @46e7d25 did not list the ORN_* types. I didn't re-open it today: CAN'T VERIFY today.
